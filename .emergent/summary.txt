<product_requirements>
The ALORIA AGENCY app digitalizes immigration case management for Canada and France, using JWT auth, a Bleu Nuit dark theme, and French UI, with Manager, Employee, and Client dashboards. Initial features included user management, chat, and visitor management. V2 added role hierarchy, client payment declaration (Manager confirms), intelligent search, and SuperAdmin capabilities. V3 introduced balance/withdrawal management, enhanced payments (PDF invoices), SuperAdmin activity history, UI harmonization, and a visitor contact form with source of knowledge and employee referral. Email automation for key events (prospect form, user creation, case status) and client payment history persistence were also crucial. The app underwent a complete mobile-first responsiveness refactor.

- **Email Notifications**: For every stage of prospect workflow (initial contact, assignment, RDV, client conversion) and user/profile creation.
